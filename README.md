# 🏥 Hospital Patient Care & Analytics Dashboard

An interactive healthcare analytics dashboard built in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze clinical workflows, financial treatment distributions, patient demographics, and department loads across **1,000+ patient admissions**.

---

![Hospital Patient Care Dashboard](HOSPITAL%20PATIENT%20CARE%20&%20ANANLYTICS.jpeg)

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Treatment Cost:** $41M
* **Total Admissions:** 1,000+ patients
* **Total Discharges:** 1,000+
* **Total Doctors on Duty:** 10
* **Average Treatment Cost:** $41.35K per patient
* **Average Age:** 44.53 years
* **Average Length of Stay (ALOS):** 8.10 days

---

## 🏢 Business Problems & Objectives
1. **Resource & Capacity Management:** Identifying departmental bottlenecks and bed occupancy patterns through Average Length of Stay (ALOS) metrics.
2. **Clinical Financial Efficiency:** Correlating treatment expenditure across medical departments with admitted disease categories to curb unnecessary medical costs.
3. **Revenue Stream Transparency:** Monitoring patient billing channels (Insurance, Cash, Card, UPI) to minimize payment processing latency and bad debts.

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Insights
* **Department Cost Concentration:** Cardiology and Neurology represent the highest share of clinical treatment expenditure (~$10.8M and ~$6.9M respectively), driven by specialized interventions.
* **Patient Demographics:** Balanced gender distribution (~50.1% Male vs. ~49.9% Female) with an average cohort age of ~44.5 years.
* **Payment Modalities:** Insurance claims account for the largest payment method share (~51.9%), requiring automated claim validation workflows.
* **Disease Volume:** Fractures, Migraines, and Arthritis form the highest admission volumes, demanding adequate orthopedic and general medicine staffing.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Implementation
* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Star schema mapping patient records, departmental costs, and clinical categories
* **DAX Measures:** Custom measures for Average Length of Stay, average treatment cost per cohort, and dynamic admission aggregation
